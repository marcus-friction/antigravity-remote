Remote Client for Antigravity is an independent, open-source mobile companion client for accessing https://antigravity.google.com on Android phones and tablets.

KEY FEATURES
• Cross-Workstation Thread Inbox & Projects: Monitor pending tool/question approvals (including subagent prompts), execution errors, and unread completions across all connected workstations, browse conversations grouped by server project, create or add remote projects, and switch threads instantly with preserved scroll position and one-tap Mark read.
• Adaptive Tablet & Foldable Layout: Uses a docked side-by-side navigation sidebar on wide tablet and unfolded screens, and automatically opens the drawer on phones whenever archiving or navigating leaves an empty workspace screen.
• Guided Setup & Opt-In Permissions: A quick intro tour explains navigation and lets you explicitly choose whether to enable on-device Action Notifications and optional Background Keep-Alive (both Off by default, adjustable anytime in Settings).
• Proactive & Locked-Screen Action Notifications: Opt in to receive live color-coded local notification cards (with each thread's project name) when an agent needs approval, asks a question, or finishes — including while your phone is locked when background keep-alive is enabled — deep-linking straight to each thread or Thread Inbox.
• Live Quota & Usage Tracking: Check plan credits, 5-hour and weekly quota pools, and per-model reset countdowns directly in the sidebar Usage tab.
• Persistent Session Management & Bounded Opt-In Keep-Alive: Sign in once to resume your workspace across restarts, and optionally keep active workstation connections and status updates running across app switches and screen locks (Off by default; configurable in Settings to Off, 5m, 15m, 30m, or 60m) with a Stop Keep-Alive notification button.
• Instant CLI & Desktop Handoff: Paste any /remote-control output link (https://antigravity.google.com/r/<instance-id>), instance ID, or open antigravity-remote://r/<instance-id> deep links directly into your active mobile session.
• Adaptive Mobile Keyboard & Viewport: Automatically adjusts the viewport and keeps the bottom chat prompt visible above the software keyboard on Android 15/16 edge-to-edge screens.
• One-Tap Session Hygiene: Unmounts the WebView and wipes all cookies, DOM storage, and WebView cache cleanly whenever you sign out.
• Strict Origin Security: Restricts in-app navigation strictly to Antigravity and first-party Google authentication hosts while opening external HTTPS links in your default system browser.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Zero Off-Device Telemetry: Operates no backend servers, analytics SDKs, or advertising trackers.
• Local-Only Storage: Session cookies remain strictly on your device with Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer disabled.
• Open Source (MIT License): Source code and security architecture are publicly auditable at https://github.com/marcus-friction/antigravity-remote.
